Open items

Both hardened-config timeout flags from 8/17 are now re-isolated. go-flutter/bookmarks-app (pair 4) was contention — a clean-restart rerun passed in 18 min with a real 328-line diff. dart/sequential-runner (pair 5) is not contention or a genuine settlement-speed problem: three clean-isolation reruns, three different loop shapes, still 0/3 recovered. Shell-masked pipe (Task 4) → shape-sensitive alternation (confirmed the widened fingerprint detects a real stall live, sameFailure reaching 4, stalled firing 33 times — but trace-only, nothing could act) → argument-echoed-into-output alternation (same day, with two more detection fixes and an opt-in intercept action live): the model's two looping commands' literal filter arguments got echoed into the output text itself, so even the category-based fingerprint saw different text every time and never sustained a streak. Deliberately not chased with a fourth same-day rerun — the real fix now looks like it needs cycle detection over a trailing window, not another fingerprinting refinement.

Background-process kills on long unattended /goal runs — root-caused (Task 5) to Pi's client-side 5-minute idle HTTP timeout, not the proxy's 30-minute total cap. Disabling it (httpIdleTimeoutMs: 0) was tested against the exact reproduction that killed two prior forced-compaction runs (Task 8): both survived. Not a fully controlled A/B (the precise trigger — a gap over 5 minutes — wasn't reproduced independently of compaction), but the first time this recipe hasn't killed the process, matching the root-cause prediction.

Corrective-follow-up mechanism resolved by narrowing, not by fixing delivery mode (8/19). The reviewer-truncation investigation found followUp messages structurally can't interrupt an active tool-calling loop — a queued correction can sit undelivered forever. Rather than switch to deliverAs: "steer" (untested, its own validation question), in-band corrective nudging was removed entirely from quality-gate.ts and cross-model-review.ts; both now do pure reporting via trace metadata. Live-confirmed end to end: a 4th go/lru-cache rerun post-decoupling shows zero injected followUp messages, the reviewer's finding landing purely in trace metadata, and the session settling honestly as a real, cleanly-reported failure. goal-gate.ts's followUp calls were deliberately left alone — unattended builds have no human to hand a trace to mid-run.

The malformed-verdict reviewer failure flagged 8/18 is root-caused and fixed: token-cap truncation (finish_reason: "length") on a long self-correcting analysis field, not a parsing regression. Fix: explicit max_tokens: 8192, a brevity-bounded prompt, and a one-shot stricter retry on truncation — verified via the planted-bug battery (still 15/15 catches, 0/9 false positives) and a direct replay of the original truncating diff (now clean in 5.8s, was 202.9s/178.8s failing).

quality-gate.ts overhead (Task 3): no current-code fix justified. Post-decoupling, three live logs each show exactly one verification trace costing 842ms/643ms/2,398ms against 110s/271s/120s total run time — small relative to total, though a fresh live paired battery is still needed for a real overhead number untangled from the 8/17 contention window.

TypeScript/JS battery coverage landed (Task 7): a live javascript/lru-cache baseline/harness pair — baseline failed the hidden test, harness passed after its own settlement check rejected a first bad attempt and the reviewer independently flagged the same bug before a corrected diff settled clean.

New from Task 8: the raw session_compact extension event still never fires (5-for-5 across all attempts to date), now precisely characterized: it's gated on the extension runner being alive, which appears to be torn down by the time trailing post-agent_end compaction runs in pi -p mode. Landing compaction genuinely inside the active loop — not just chronologically mid-goal — looks structurally required to ever observe it fire; not yet confirmed with a task engineered to force enough corrective rounds to test that directly.
